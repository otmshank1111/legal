Privacy Policy

Brand Analytics Dashboard · Last updated 31 August 2026

1. Scope

Brand Analytics Dashboard is a private internal reporting tool operated by KK SP & All Ghost Labs. It has no public users and collects no data from anyone other than the operator's own social media accounts.

2. What we access

When the operator authorizes one of their own accounts, the software may read, through the platform's official API and only within the scopes granted:

  • Basic profile information for the authorized account, such as its display name, avatar, and account identifier
  • Aggregate account statistics, such as follower count, following count, total likes, and video count
  • A list of the authorized account's own public posts and their metadata

We do not access private messages, contacts, viewer identities, or any data about other people's accounts.

3. What we do with it

Data is written to local storage on the operator's own machine and used to produce internal weekly performance reports. That is the only purpose.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose the data to any third party.
  • We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or training machine learning models.
  • We do not combine it with data purchased or obtained from other sources.
  • We do not post, message, or take any action on a connected account.

5. Storage and security

Metrics are stored locally on hardware controlled by the operator. Access tokens are held in environment configuration that is excluded from version control and never committed to a repository. Backups are private.

6. Retention

Historical metrics are retained so that trends can be compared over time. Access tokens are discarded when authorization is revoked or when they expire and are not renewed.

7. Revoking access

The operator can revoke the software's access at any time from the connected platform's own settings, for example in TikTok under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions. Revocation takes effect immediately and stops all further data collection.

8. Children

The software is not directed at children and collects no data from them.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
